Engineering Interpretation

Physical principles represented in the HYROVISION3D simulator.

Thrust Generation

Propeller rotation transfers momentum to surrounding water. The resulting reaction force generates thrust.

Torque and Shaft Input

Shaft torque represents rotational loading. Mechanical input is calculated from torque and angular velocity.

Blade Pressure

Pressure differences across propeller blade surfaces contribute to thrust. The current colour field is illustrative, not obtained from CFD.

Cavitation

Vapour cavities may form when local absolute pressure reaches the water vapour pressure. Collapse in higher-pressure regions can cause noise, vibration and erosion.

Engineering Simulation Notice: HYROVISION3D is an educational marine propulsion simulator. The thrust, torque and efficiency model uses illustrative open-water coefficients. The blade-pressure colours, water particles, tip vortices and vapour cavities are graphical approximations rather than CFD solutions. The cavitation assessment uses assumed environmental conditions and estimated local-pressure coefficients. Results require independent verification before use in vessel design, machinery selection or operational decisions.
